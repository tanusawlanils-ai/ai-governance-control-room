2. Performance

Incident signal card and offline validation table
Incident signal (92% → 31%)
The live, single-event confidence trace from this incident's camera detector — not a validation statistic, and not comparable to precision or recall.
Elapsed / Measure / AEB threshold
How long the confidence collapse took, what is being measured, and the fixed confidence level automatic braking requires before it will arm.
Offline validation table
Precision and recall measured across many test cases per lighting/weather condition — a separate, aggregate measure used only to contextualize the incident.
Precision
Of all detections the model flagged, the share that were correct.
Recall
Of all pedestrians actually present, the share the model correctly detected.
Weakest-condition flag
Marks the night/rain row, which matches this incident's conditions and has the lowest recall in the validation set.
All four validation rows: daylight/clear, night/clear, daylight/rain, night/rain
Contextualizing sentence
The table is followed by an explicit statement that offline rates contextualize the incident but do not recreate it — the two measures are never implied to be the same thing.

3. Safety framing

SOTIF vs. functional-safety framing and the hazard classification selector
ISO 21448 / SOTIF
Safety of the Intended Functionality — covers hazards that arise even when every component works as designed, which is the framing this incident falls under.
ISO 26262
Functional safety for road vehicles — applies once a fault or malfunction in a component is evidenced. Not established for this incident.
Severity / Exposure / Controllability selector
An interactive hazard-classification control. Severity is harm if the hazard is realised, exposure is how often the condition occurs, and controllability is whether the driver or others can avoid harm.
Provisional review posture & illustrative ASIL rating
A live-computed recommendation and combined risk rating that update as the S/E/C selection changes — illustrative only, not a certified determination.
Demo disclaimer
"This is a simplified teaching view, not a safety case or certification decision" is shown persistently on this tab.
The S/E/C selector with the resulting posture and illustrative ASIL badge

4. Evidence chain

Traceability chain, coverage bar, and the evidence inspector
Traceability chain
The seven-step record chain from hazard to governance decision, each step showing its status (received/missing) and accountable owner. Click any step to inspect it.
Coverage & traceability bar
The share of chain records received, computed live from the record data — not hardcoded.
Ownership pipeline
The sequence of teams responsible for the chain, from Requirements through to the Governance Board.
Evidence inspector
A detailed view of the selected record: type, accountable owner, source, downstream recipient, status, and (where applicable) full record contents and a concrete next step.
Demo received/missing toggle
Lets you flip a record's status for demonstration and watch coverage, the open-gaps list, and the board summary recompute live.
The full record-contents view, shown here for the two records with rich detail: REQ-AEB-017 and EVD-0417
Record contents

Concrete next step: the single accountable action tied to the selected record
Concrete next step

Open gaps panel
Lists every missing record by ID and title, and states plainly that sign-off cannot complete until they close.
Governance actions tracker and supplier compliance summary
Governance actions

5. Board decision

The three decision options and the resulting rationale
Decision options (Approve / Approve with Conditions / Block)
The three positions the board can take. Selecting one rewrites the rationale below to match.
Board rationale
An honest weighing of safety uncertainty, evidence gaps, deployment scope, and schedule/cost for the selected option — never generic success messaging. All three options (Approve, Approve with Conditions, Block) have a full, distinct rationale; Approve with Conditions is the default recommendation.
Export artefact (PDF)
Opens the browser's print dialog so the current state of the case can be saved as a PDF record.
The four weighing factors shown for every decision option
Safety uncertainty / Evidence gaps / Deployment scope / Schedule & cost
The four factors weighed for whichever option is selected — the same four factors, argued differently, for all three options.
Conditions on record, shown for Approve with Conditions
Conditions on record
The specific commitments attached to a conditional approval, each with an owner and a trigger for re-review. Approve has no conditions (risk transfers outright); Block requires full re-validation before resubmission.
