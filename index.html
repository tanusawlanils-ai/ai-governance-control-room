How this hazard is classified

Synthetic / illustrative

The camera and AI were working normally, but still failed to reliably see a pedestrian in low light and rain. That's called a performance limitation — a system doing its job but not well enough in a tough condition. ISO 21448 (SOTIF)Safety Of The Intended Functionality — a standard for when a system works exactly as designed, but still isn't good enough in some real-world situations, like an AI seeing worse in rain. Common for AI/sensor-based features. covers this kind of AI/sensor limitation. It's different from a broken part, which is covered by ISO 26262The main functional-safety standard for road vehicles. It mainly covers components that fail or break — not a working AI system that's just less reliable in a hard condition. instead.
