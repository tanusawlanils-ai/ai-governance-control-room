NARRATIVE · SYNTHETIC RECONSTRUCTION

At 23:41 local time, VH-042 approached a marked crosswalk in light rain. The onboard camera detector briefly reported pedestrian detection confidence{{ tips.confidence.text }}of 92%, then fell to 31% within roughly 400 ms as glare and rain degraded the signal. The AEB arming threshold{{ tips.aeb.text }}of 50% was not reached, so automatic braking did not engage. The driver intervened manually and the vehicle stopped short of the crosswalk. No contact occurred — the event is logged as a near-miss and routed to governance review.
